# Credits

Lantern Knight is built from free (CC0) art and audio and permissively licensed libraries.

## Art (CC0)
- **KayKit Character Pack: Adventures 1.0**, **KayKit Character Pack: Skeletons 1.0**, **KayKit Dungeon Remastered 1.0** by Kay Lousberg (kaylousberg.com). The knight, the greataxe (from the Barbarian), every skeleton enemy and boss, and the whole dungeon.
- **Fantasy Props MegaKit (Standard)** by Quaternius (quaternius.com), via OpenGameArt. Candles, stands and props where KayKit has nothing. Textures downscaled to 512 px.
- **Input Prompts** and **Kenney Fonts** by Kenney (kenney.nl), via the OpenGameArt mirror.

## Audio (CC0)
- **50 RPG Sound Effects (RPG Audio)** by Kenney, via OpenGameArt: footsteps, armor, doors, coins, UI.
- **75 CC0 Breaking/Falling/Hit SFX** and **80 CC0 Creature SFX** by rubberduck (OpenGameArt): impacts, bones, screams, roars.
- **20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)** by StarNinjas (OpenGameArt): swings and parry clashes.
- **Dark Cavern Ambient** by Paul Wortmann (OpenGameArt): exploration music.
- **Basilisk Boss Battle Loop** by Beardalaxy (OpenGameArt): boss music.
- Generated placeholders (made for this project, CC0): lantern crackle loop, whoosh. See TODO_ASSETS.md.
